# creative-direction

Create 2–3 concise directions and compare them against audience, differentiation, content needs,
brand evidence, bilingual copy-safe zones, feasibility, and performance. Ask the owner to select
one. Record only the selected direction as `creative-direction.json` with `status: approved`,
`approval.status`, `reviewed_by`, and a note. Never treat a reference as permission to copy.

The approved contract states one central idea; desktop and mobile hero compositions; light,
palette, type/material cues and safe regions; documentary versus conceptual boundaries;
prohibited elements; and opaque glass fallbacks. Seal it and request creative-director review.
Do not generate assets before approval.
